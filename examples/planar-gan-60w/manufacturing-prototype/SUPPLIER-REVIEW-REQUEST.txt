PS-GAN-60W - FIVE UNITS - ENGINEERING REVIEW REQUEST, NOT AN ORDER
Native revision: A10 schematic / A8 hardware, audited 10 October 2026.

Do not manufacture this revision. Manufacturing-issue-register.csv identifies
design corrections that must be completed before final CAM and assembly review.
This document has not been sent to JLCPCB. It defines the questions and files
needed to obtain an actual turnkey offer after those corrections.

Requested delivery scope
Five converters with 73 top SMT placements and two top-inserted THT headers
per board; no purchased electronics on the bottom. Transformer windings are
PCB copper. A ferrite half goes below the board; that is a separate mechanical
operation, not bottom-side SMT.

Review file set
- Gerbers-PROTOTYPE.zip: eight copper layers, masks, top paste, legend,

- BOM-JLCPCB.csv: exact MPN in Comment, exact C-code, manufacturer and quantity.
- CPL-JLCPCB-review.csv: native footprint anchors / rotations, not approved

  in the same lower-left datum (X right, Y up); do not import it as P&P data.
- Assembly-top.pdf, mounting-holes.csv, the native KiCad project and issue
  register. Check the release's SHA256 manifest before quoting final files.

Fabrication and panel acceptance
1. Supply an approved eight-layer heavy-copper stack. The present design uses
   nominal 1.6 mm and 70 um copper on all layers. JLC081622-1080 is only a
   candidate: the quote UI lists 1.655 mm actual thickness and 61 um inner
   copper. Engineering must first reconcile the magnetic model, native stack
   and finished-thickness / copper tolerances. No silent substitution.
2. Preserve the final 64 x 56 mm chamfered outline, 55 x 47 mm M3 pattern and
   three CNC core openings. Agree laminate grade, finished thickness, routing
   tolerance, material and plating acceptance in the fabrication drawing.
3. Supply handling-panel CAM for approval. This outline is smaller than the
   published 70 x 70 mm Standard PCBA envelope. Use routed tabs with adequate
   edge rails, tooling and fiducials; allow opposing connector overhang and
   soldering access. A roughly 90 x 76 mm one-up frame is a budgeting envelope
   only. Final tab positions must clear copper, mounting holes, ferrite slots
   and populated parts; do not V-score through chamfers or mounting features.
4. Resin-fill, copper-cap and planarize ALL 221 interconnect drills of 0.30 mm.
   These are PTH drill tools T1 (215 vias) and T2 (six T1 winding transitions).
   The transitions are not component insertion holes. Confirm this explicitly
   in CAM: automatic via detection may distinguish vias from plated pads.
   Leave PTH tool T3 (four 1.60 mm connector holes) and all four 3.20 mm NPTH
   mounting holes open. Tenting is not a substitute for plated-over fill.
5. Confirm the approved U1 mask/stencil after footprint correction, including
   the 2 oz mask bridge process. Do not silently delete the narrow mask dams.
   No changes to winding turns, spacing or keepouts without engineering review.

Electronics assembly acceptance
6. Confirm all 43 exact C-codes, package identities, available factory stock,
   purchase minima, attrition and substitutions policy for 375 fitted parts.
   Recheck low-stock F1 C3177336 and L2 C2047750. No alternate 32 V fuse.
7. Approve physical package centers, pin 1 / cathode and rotation for every
   polarized component using pad overlays, not a universal angular correction.
   The current U1 library origin is offset from its package center. Confirm
   connector insertion orientation and pin 2 positive / pin 1 return on both.
8. Review moisture handling, reflow profile and stencil for the actual parts
   and heavy-copper assembly. Supply process-compatible apertures after the
   GaN footprint repair. Inspect U1, U4, Q1 and Q2 hidden joints by X-ray and
   agree voiding/acceptance limits with engineering rather than inventing a
   generic acceptable percentage.
9. Confirm the two WJ2EDGRC headers' separate THT operation. Their 4 mm tails
   protrude through the nominal 1.6 mm PCB. Agree wave/selective/hand process,
   lead protrusion, clearance, solder fill and panel removal sequence.

Mechanical assembly and accessory acceptance
10. Confirm whether you can source through DigiKey/global sourcing or accept
    consignment of 10 x TDK B66285G0050X187 and 10 x B66286A2000X000, then fit
    two halves and two clips per PCB after soldering and depanelization.
    Quote the operation and any attrition separately. A parts catalog listing
    is not acceptance of a core-fitting operation.
11. Fit only the exact gap suffix: each half has a 0.05 mm center gap; paired
    nominal total gap is 0.10 mm. Seat clean outer-leg faces; no grinding,
    spacer, adhesive in mating faces, chipped cores or forced PCB bending.
    Final work instructions require a real tolerance/fit trial and measured
    magnetics acceptance limits, currently outstanding.
12. Two C71370 mating plugs per converter are external accessories. Confirm
    an explicitly accepted plug-fitting operation if they are to ship fitted.
    They are not in the current PCB BOM/CPL. Do not assume loose parts ship
    with PCBA. Otherwise procure all 10 mating plugs separately from LCSC.
13. Four M3 fasteners/standoffs per unit are not in the BOM and are not required
    for standalone PCBA delivery. If an installed assembly is requested, agree
    hardware separately. Preserve the exposed-substrate hole pattern; use
    nonconductive supports with contact diameter <=6.4 mm and adequate ferrite
    underside clearance. Provide protection for the ferrite during shipment.

Test and commercial acceptance
14. Bare-board net test, AOI, SPI and X-ray do not prove converter function.
    Electrical release is on hold. Do not perform a blind 48 V / 60 W power-up.
    Engineering must validate startup, fault protection, surge margin, output
    regulation, synchronous timing, capacitor ripple, magnetics and temperature
    before issuing a controlled production-test specification.
15. Return an itemized five-unit quote: PCB/panel/finish/fill, parts plus excess,
    SMT/setup/stencil/feeders/fixtures, THT, X-ray, core/clip and plug operations,
    test engineering/test per unit, depanelization, packing, shipping, tax and
    duties. State accepted exceptions, lead time, final deliverables and whether
    the result is electronics-only PCBA or a mechanically complete converter.
