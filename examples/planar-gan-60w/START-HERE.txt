PS-GAN-60W — A4 schematic / A2 hardware — KiCad 10 engineering prototype
Open Schematic.pdf for the clearer, visibly wired circuit with directly wired OC sensing and bias circuitry, attached supply arrows, aligned labels and no four-way connections. The 64 x 56 mm board uses 23% less outline area than A1; headers, mating plugs and wires add to the envelope. Open Review.html for the design review and interactive copper-layer viewer.
Open kicad/PS-GAN-60W.kicad_pro in KiCad. Both schematic and PCB are editable, with local symbols, footprints and 3D models.

Board: 64 x 56 mm, eight layers, all electronic components on top. Four flyback-style M3 mounting holes on a 55 x 47 mm pattern; opposite-edge 5.08 mm headers with removable screw-terminal plugs. See PER-DESIGN-STUDY.md for the MIT research and decisions. The pattern differs from the flyback example; hole geometry is reused exactly. Ferrite core straddles the PCB. Fully routed; ERC and DRC report zero violations, zero unrouted connections and zero schematic-parity issues. These are CAD checks, not hardware qualification.

Schematic.pdf and PCB-layers.pdf are native KiCad documentation. manufacturing-prototype contains Gerbers, drills, electronics BOM and a CPL requiring JLCPCB placement-preview verification. Read manufacturing-prototype/READ-BEFORE-ORDER.txt before using these files.

All 75 purchased electronic components have exact JLCPCB catalog IDs; 41 unique codes. Electronics-only catalog allowance is $23.68 at first listed price tiers. Two external mating plugs add $0.39; two DigiKey core halves and two clips add approximately $6.88 in the retrieved listing, for $30.95 in parts, excluding PCB, assembly, setup, attrition, shipping, tax and minimum quantities. Stock and prices can change.

Planar Studio inspiration: examples/planar-flyback-5w from repository commit 71f6f4db9c1a7172bddca8c9c934d3a8ece2a481. This design changes topology, turns, control and power stage; it is not a power-scaled copy. T1.planar.json is an importable FOUR-section seed (P,S,S,P). The final board parallels each secondary section on an additional layer; see T1-artwork.json and T1-windings.kicad_pcb. Importing the seed alone does not recreate all six final winding layers.

Validation: evidence includes ERC, DRC, pin-by-pin manifest/netlist comparison, winding-contact sampling, all eight native copper views, magnetic assumptions and first-harmonic LLC calculations. No switching simulation or hardware test has been performed. 60 W, regulation, efficiency and temperature remain targets.
