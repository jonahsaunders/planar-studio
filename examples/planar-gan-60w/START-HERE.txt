PS-GAN-60W — KiCad 10 engineering prototype
Open Review.html for the design review and interactive copper-layer viewer.
Open kicad/PS-GAN-60W.kicad_pro in KiCad. Both schematic and PCB are editable, with local symbols, footprints and 3D models.

Board: 80 x 58 mm, eight layers, all electronic components on top. Four flyback-style M3 mounting holes on a 71 x 49 mm pattern; opposite-edge polarized XT30 power connectors. See PER-DESIGN-STUDY.md for the MIT research and decisions. The pattern differs from the flyback example; hole geometry is reused exactly. Ferrite core straddles the PCB. Fully routed; ERC and DRC report zero violations, zero unrouted connections and zero schematic-parity issues. These are CAD checks, not hardware qualification.

Schematic.pdf and PCB-layers.pdf are native KiCad documentation. manufacturing-prototype contains Gerbers, drills, electronics BOM and a CPL requiring JLCPCB placement-preview verification. Read manufacturing-prototype/READ-BEFORE-ORDER.txt before using these files.

All 75 purchased electronic components have exact JLCPCB catalog IDs; 42 unique codes. Electronics-only catalog allowance is $24.30 at first listed price tiers. Two DigiKey core halves and two clips add approximately $6.88 in the retrieved listing, for $31.18 in parts, excluding PCB, assembly, setup, attrition, shipping, tax and minimum quantities. Stock and prices can change.

Planar Studio inspiration: examples/planar-flyback-5w from repository commit 71f6f4db9c1a7172bddca8c9c934d3a8ece2a481. This design changes topology, turns, control and power stage; it is not a power-scaled copy. T1.planar.json is an importable FOUR-section seed (P,S,S,P). The final board parallels each secondary section on an additional layer; see T1-artwork.json and T1-windings.kicad_pcb. Importing the seed alone does not recreate all six final winding layers.

Validation: evidence includes ERC, DRC, pin-by-pin manifest/netlist comparison, winding-contact sampling, all eight native copper views, magnetic assumptions and first-harmonic LLC calculations. No switching simulation or hardware test has been performed. 60 W, regulation, efficiency and temperature remain targets.

scripts/export_review.py regenerates exports with an ERC/DRC gate using kicad-cli. scripts/audit_windings.py and final_audit.py require KiCad's pcbnew Python. engineering_review.py requires numpy. regenerate_magnetics.mjs requires Node and a Planar Studio checkout passed as its first argument at the commit above; it regenerates winding review files, not the edited main PCB.

KiStack workflow used: schematic, BOM, PCB, symbol/footprint and export skills. Manufacturer and JLCPCB/LCSC sources were used directly with the user's approval. See THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.txt for source and CAD-license information.
